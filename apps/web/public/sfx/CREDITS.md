# Sound effect credits

Every sound in this folder comes from **Kenney** (www.kenney.nl) and is released under
**Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Each pack's `License.txt` reads: "Created/distributed by Kenney (www.kenney.nl) … License: (Creative Commons Zero, CC0) …
This content is free to use in personal, educational and commercial projects. Support us by crediting Kenney or
www.kenney.nl (this is not mandatory)." Thank you, Kenney!

**Where the files came from.** The build box couldn't reach kenney.nl directly; the connection timed out. So the
original files were taken from an unmodified mirror of Kenney's packs, including their `License.txt` files: the
GitHub repo `CraigBuilds/kenney_assets_v2` (folder `assets/Audio/<pack>/`), downloaded through the jsDelivr CDN.
The "pack page" links below are Kenney's official download pages for the same packs.

**Processing** (ffmpeg): leading silence trimmed so the sound starts on the hit frame, downmixed to mono at 44.1 kHz,
encoded as Ogg Vorbis (q3) and AAC 64 kb/s `.m4a` (for Safari). A few long sounds were cut short with a fade-out (noted below).

Packs: Impact Sounds 1.0 (2019), Foley Sounds 1.0 (2014), RPG Audio 1.0 (2014), Sci-Fi Sounds 1.0 (2020),
Digital Audio 1.0 (2012), Interface Sounds 1.0 (2020), Music Jingles 1.1 (2014).
